The Legacy / The Story

Formed in Seattle in 1987, Alice in Chains forged a singular path in rock history by pairing heavy metal riffs with the haunting, trademark vocal harmonies of guitarist Jerry Cantrell and the late Layne Staley. Propelled by groundbreaking releases like Facelift, Dirt, and the record-breaking acoustic EP Jar of Flies, the band became a cornerstone of the grunge era. Following Staley's tragic passing, Cantrell, drummer Sean Kinney, and bassist Mike Inez reunited in 2005, ushering in a triumphant second chapter alongside co-lead vocalist William DuVall with critically acclaimed albums like Black Gives Way to Blue and Rainier Fog. With over 30 million records sold and a legacy that continues to shape heavy music, Alice in Chains stands as one of the most resilient and influential rock acts of all time.
