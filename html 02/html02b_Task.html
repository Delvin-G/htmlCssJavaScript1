Ultimate Party Planning Guide

Step-by-step instructions and checklists for a perfect event

Planning Your Party

Timeline

  1. Choose a date and venue(4-6 weeks before)
  2. Create and send invitations (3 weeks before)
  3. Plan the menu and purchase ingredients (1 week before)
  4. Decorate and set up (1 day before)
  5. Enjoy the party! (Day of event)

Guest Essentials

  • Comfortable seating
  • Good lighting
  • Beverage station
  • Music or entertainment
  • Clean restrooms

Food Preparation

  1. prepare apps
    • Arrange on platters
    • Chill until serving
  2. cook main dishes
    • Set oven temperature
    • Monitor cooking times
  3. prepare desserts
    • Bake ahead if possible
    • Keep refrigerated
  4. set up drink station

Party Supplies Checklist

Plates: Paper or ceramic, 1 per guest plus extras
Cups: 2-3 per guest (for multiple drinks)
Napkins: 4-5 per guest
Utensils: Forks, spoons, knives for each guest
Trash bags: 3-4 large bags for cleanup
